Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us, especially regarding sensitive personal hygiene data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Image Data (No Upload)

All image processing and analysis are performed locally on your device using your web browser's built-in capabilities. We do not collect, store, upload, or share any of your photos or camera feeds. Your images never leave your device.

2. Permissions

We request the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Required to capture the image for local analysis.
  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback for visually impaired users.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party image processing APIs. If your device uses a built-in text-to-speech (TTS) engine or screen reader, the text data (e.g., "Clean" or "Please wipe again") may be processed by your device's operating system. We do not transmit this text data to our servers.

4. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your images or personal data, there is no database that can be breached. Your usage is completely anonymous.
